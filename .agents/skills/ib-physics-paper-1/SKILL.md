# IB DP Physics Paper 1

First assessment 2025. Paper 1 is worth 36% at both levels. Complete both booklets in one uninterrupted session. Calculators are permitted and candidates need a clean Physics data booklet.

| Level | 1A multiple choice | 1B data-based | Total | Combined time |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| SL | 25 | 20 | 45 | 90 min |
| HL | 40 | 20 | 60 | 120 min |

Paper 1A tests SL material at SL and includes additional HL material at HL. Paper 1B assesses interpretation of data and experimental skills in context. Answer all questions.

## Mark and give feedback

Read [Paper 1 marking](references/marking.md). Establish 1A or 1B, level, and the paper or practice set from the supplied material. For a real paper, match its session, level and variant or paper code before using an answer key or markscheme. Grade the work shown. If only one section or selected questions are supplied, report the score for that scope. If the scheme is unavailable, offer a clearly labeled independent key or estimated marks wherever the questions are readable.

## Solve, practise and plan

- For 1A, show the shortest sound reasoning. Use a physical principle, dimensions, limiting cases or an order-of-magnitude check as appropriate. Explain the selected distractor when diagnosing an error. For 1B, read the axes, units, scales and error bars first. Show graph read-offs and calculation steps, and tie conclusions to the data and its uncertainty.
- For a study plan, separate 1A concept and calculation errors from 1B graphing, uncertainty and experimental-design errors. Use a timed diagnostic, targeted drills, then a retest. For a full mock, a starting split is 45/40/5 minutes for SL or 65/50/5 for HL (1A/1B/check). Adjust from the student's timed results.
- For original practice, label it unofficial and match the level. Give each 1A item four options with one defensible best answer. Build 1B questions around a usable dataset or graph and allocate marks to distinct steps. Independently solve each item, check rounding, graph tolerances and the totals, and give the key or markscheme separately.
