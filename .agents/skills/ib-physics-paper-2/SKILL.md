# IB DP Physics Paper 2

First assessment 2025. Paper 2 uses short-answer and extended-response questions and is worth 44% at both levels. Calculators are permitted and candidates need a clean Physics data booklet. Answer all questions.

| Level | Scope | Time | Marks |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| SL | Standard level content | 90 min | 50 |
| HL | Standard level and additional higher level content | 150 min | 90 |

The updated guide gives SL 50 marks. If an older specimen or practice set is supplied, use the marks on the actual questions and check the accompanying scheme for discrepancies.

## Mark and give feedback

Read [Paper 2 marking](references/marking.md) before scoring. Match the question paper, student responses and markscheme by level, session and variant or paper code. Inspect diagrams and handwritten working where available. Apply question-specific marking points and explain which evidence earned each mark. Where a matching scheme is unavailable, solve the readable questions independently and identify any provisional credit. Keep selected-question totals separate from full-paper totals.

## Write responses and plan practice

- Follow the command term. For calculations, show the governing relation, substitutions with consistent units and the result. For derivations, show the intermediate steps and state assumptions where they matter. For explanations, name the principle and connect it causally to the requested effect. For sketches, label axes or components and show the required features.
- Turn recurring losses into drills on the underlying skill, such as choosing the system boundary, resolving vectors, linking equations across subparts or explaining a trend. For timed practice, reserve a short review window and budget the remaining time roughly by the printed marks. Revisit stuck parts after securing the accessible marks. Use a fresh timed set to check transfer.
- For original practice, match the level and use structured contexts that test both application and reasoning. Provide all needed data and diagrams, choose command terms that fit the intended evidence, and check that each subpart can be solved with the information and data booklet available. Keep the original question paper and its worked markscheme separate. Verify the physics, alternative methods, dependencies and mark totals.
