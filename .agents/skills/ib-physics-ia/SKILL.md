# IB DP Physics IA

First assessment 2025. The IA is one individual scientific investigation report, with the same requirements at SL and HL. It is worth 20% and is marked out of 24 by the teacher, with external moderation by the IB. The guide allocates about 10 hours of teaching time. The report must be at most 3,000 words.

## Plan and write

Read [IA writing and planning](references/writing.md) and the [section checklist](references/checklist.md). If the student supplies a school template, marking grid or exemplar, follow its conventions where they do not conflict with the current IB requirements, and check whether it uses the pre-2025 criteria. Start from the student's stage, interests, available apparatus or data and deadline. Shape a research question around a measurable relationship, a defined system and a testable physical model. For planning requests, give the student a sequence of actions with a pilot or data feasibility check before committing to full collection. For a draft, prioritize gaps in the chain from research question to method, data, conclusion and evaluation.

Use the student's own data, analysis and decisions. Help with outlines, calculations, feedback and clearly labeled practice examples. Keep the report's authorship with the student. Preserve missing values and citations as explicit gaps, and distinguish any simulated example from observations. Where safety, ethics or environmental issues apply, build the necessary controls and teacher review into the plan.

## Evaluate a draft

Read [IA rubric and scoring](references/rubric.md) and the [section checklist](references/checklist.md). Score Research design, Data analysis, Conclusion and Evaluation independently with best-fit whole marks out of 6 each. Cite the evidence in the student's work and explain why it fits the band. Give concrete next edits in priority order. For a partial draft, distinguish available evidence from sections still to be provided and label any estimate as provisional.
